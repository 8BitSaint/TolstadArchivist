# Tolstad Archivist: The Esoteric Ebb Knowledgebase

> "There's a rhythm to Jor's Realm, and you heard his voice. Find it at his closest Pillar. It reaches deep."

Welcome to the central repository for mapping the city of **Norvik**, the mysteries of the **City Below**, and the intricate political web of **Tolstad**. This is an Obsidian vault designed to track every clue, character, and esoteric oddity encountered in *Esoteric Ebb*.

Inspired by the depth of *Disco Elysium*, this vault aims to be the definitive completionist's guide to the game's dense lore and unforgiving mechanics.

## 🏛️ Vault Structure

The archive is organized to mirror the game's internal systems:

- **[Game World/](./Game%20World/)**: Your boots-on-the-ground records.
  - **Characters**: Every interactable NPC, their stats, and their secrets.
  - **Locations**: A geographic map of the districts and the ruins below.
  - **Events**: Documentation of world-shaking esoteric occurrences.
- **[Menus/](./Menus/)**: Verbatim game data and technical references.
  - **Journal**: A massive lore library categorized by skill (Intelligence, Wisdom, etc.).
  - **Inventory**: A Title Case catalog of every item, from the *Frog-mouth* helm to the *Pickaxe of Secrets*.
  - **Spellbook**: Technical breakdowns of your divine and arcane arsenal.
  - **Questing**: A web of quest logs and branching decision guides.
- **[Templates/](./Templates/)**: The "Source of Truth" for all entry formatting.

## 🛠️ Archival Standards

To maintain the vault's high-signal integrity, we adhere to the following rules (codified in [GEMINI.md](./GEMINI.md)):

- **Verbatim Text**: In-game text logs and descriptions are preserved in blockquotes to keep the game's voice distinct.
- **Skill-Based Lore**: Journal entries are organized by the skill check passed to earn them.
- **High-Signal Tagging**: We use tags for mechanics (#Level3, #HP, #Charisma) and avoid redundant category tags.
- **Title Case**: All entities and items use Title Case for clean, natural linking.

## 🤝 Contributing

If you are a fellow traveler of Tolstad and wish to contribute to the archive:

1. **Fork the Repo**: Work from your own fork and submit a Pull Request.
2. **Squash Your Commits**: To keep the history clean, all changes in a push should be squashed into a single, meaningful commit.
3. **Use the Templates**: Always use the files in `/Templates` when creating new entries.
4. **Link Proactively**: Link the first occurrence of any entity to its corresponding page.

## 🔍 Getting Started

1. Download and install [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/).
2. Clone this repository.
3. Open the folder as a Vault in Obsidian.
4. Enable the **Style Settings** and **Tag Wrangler** plugins (pre-configured in `.obsidian/`).

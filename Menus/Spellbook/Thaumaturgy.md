[[Transmutation]] #Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Divine]]
Range: 30 feet
Components: V
Duration: Up to 1 minute

You manifest a minor harmless wonder, a sign of supernatural power, that follows you within range. The effect allows you to commune with sacred entities and locations, granting you potential boons.

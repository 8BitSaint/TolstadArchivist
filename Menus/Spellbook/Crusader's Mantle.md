# Text
3rd-level [[evocation]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Divine]]
Range: Self (30-foot radius)
Components: V
Duration: Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Holy power radiates from you in an aura with a 30-foot radius, awakening boldness in you and all other friendly creatures. Until the spell ends, the aura moves with you, centered on you. While in an encounter, you and all allies add 1d12 to all ability checks.

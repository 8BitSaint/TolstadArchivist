# Text
2nd-level [[transmutation]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Primal]]
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (a handful of oak bark)
Duration: Concentration, up to 4 hour

For the duration your skin has a rough, bark-like appearance and you have resistance to all damage (all damage halved).

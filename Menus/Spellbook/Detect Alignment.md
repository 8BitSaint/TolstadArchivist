# Text
1st-level [[divination]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Divine]]
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

For an instant any creature of the types [[Aberration]], [[Celestial]], [[Elemental]], [[Fey]], [[Fiend]], or [[Undead]], will be clearly indicated to you. Additionally, any behold check will reveal the creature's [[alignment]] in accordance to [[Jor's Strings]].

The spell affects all creatures in the current area.

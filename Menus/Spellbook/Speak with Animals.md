# Text
1st-level [[divination]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Primal]]
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Situational, up to 10 minutes

You gain the ability to comprehend and verbally communicate with beasts for the duration of the spell. The knowledge and awareness of many beasts is limited by their #Intelligence, but at minimum, beasts can give you information about nearby locations and [[Monster|monsters]], including whatever they can perceive or have perceived within the past day.

# Info
3rd-level #Necromancy
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Divine]]
Range: 10 feet
Components: V, S, M (burning incense)
Duration: Situational, up to 10 minutes

# Effect
You grant the semblance of life and intelligence to a corpse of your choice within range, allowing it to answer the questions you pose. The corpse must still have a mouth and can't be undead. The spell fails if the corpse was the target of this spell within the last 10 days.

Until the spell ends, you can ask the corpse up to five questions. The corpse knows only what it knew in life, including the languages it knew. Answers are usually brief, cryptic, or repetitive, and the corpse is under no compulsion to offer a truthful answer if you are hostile to it or it recognizes you as an enemy. This spell doesn't return the creature's soul to its body, only its animating spirit. Thus, the corpse can't learn new information, doesn't comprehend anything that has happened since it died, and can't speculate about future events.

# Obtaining
1. Find the burnt [[Elf]] corpse in the [[Tea Shop]] to start the [[Elven Connection]] quest
2. [[Visken]] will tell you there is a Necromancer that can teach you [[Menus/Journal/Spells/Speak with Dead|Speak with Dead]] in the [[Crypt]]
3. Go to the crypt
	1. Get the [[CRYPT KEY]] from [[The Duck]] to enter the Crypt through the [[Temple of Urth]]
	2. There's a secret entrance in the hallway between [[Game World/Visitable Locations/The City Below The City|The City Below The City]] and [[Roots]]
4. Defeat the skeleton warrior
5. Turn the handle on the coffin in the wall to the left to reveal a secret room with [[Haelon Bondavol]]
6. Pass an Int DC24 check in dialog to learn the spell
	1. Can be lowered to DC22 through dialog by discovering Haelon is a lich
	2. Can gain #Advantage through dialog by having Haelon tell you about [[Menus/Journal/Esoterics/Esoteric Ebb|Esoteric Ebb]]
	3. Can gain #Advantage  by wearing the [[TINY DICTATOR]] trinket.

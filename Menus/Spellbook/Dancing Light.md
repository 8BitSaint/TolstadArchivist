# Text
[[Evocation]] #Cantrip
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Arcane]]
Range: 120 feet
Components: V, S, M (a bit of phosphorus, ildwood, or a glowglobe shard)
Duration: Concentration, up to 1 minute

You create a torch-sized light within range, making it appear as a torch, lantern, or glowing orb that hovers in the air for the duration. Whichever form you choose, the light sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.

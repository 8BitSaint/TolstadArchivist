# Text
1st-level [[transmutation]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Primal]]
Range: Touch
Components: V, S, M (a traditional 'Appelskrutt')
Duration: Instantaneous

Five apples appear in your hand and are infused with magic for the duration. A creature can use its action to eat one apple. Eating an apple restores 1 hit point, and the apple provides enough nourishment to sustain a normal creature for one day.

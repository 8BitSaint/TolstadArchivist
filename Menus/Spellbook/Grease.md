# Text
1st-level [[conjuration]]
Casting Time: 1 action
Category: [[Arcane]]
Range: 60 feet
Components: V, S, M (a bit of pork rind or butter)
Duration: Situational, up to 1 minute

Slick grease covers the ground in a 10 foot square centered on a point within range and turns it into low friction difficult terrain for the duration.

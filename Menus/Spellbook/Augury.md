# Text
2nd-level [[divination]]
Casting Time: 1 minute
Category: [[Arcane]]
Range: Self
Components: V, S, M (specially marked sticks, bones, or similar tokens worth at least 25 gp)
Duration: Instantaneous

By perfoming a divination ritual, you receive an omen from an otherwordly entity about the results of a specific course of action that you plan to take. If you recieve WEAL, your next dice check will almost always be a success. If you recieve WOE, your next dice check will almost always be a failure.

If you cast the spell two or more times before completing your next long rest, there is a increasing chance of recieving a WOE response. The effects of WEAL or WOE also end on a long rest.

## Text
Five rulers of the five [[Norvik]] districts. They are the [[Coinlord]], [[Homelord]], [[Shieldlord]], [[Foodlord]], and [[Lawlord]]. Each has a unique role in the city's bureaucracy. Sometimes the #Title s switch between districts, but generally [[The Housing District]]s holds the Homelord, and [[The Palace]] holds the Lawlord.

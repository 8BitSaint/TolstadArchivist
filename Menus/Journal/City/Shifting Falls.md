## Text
A deep cavernous part of the [[City Below]], found underneath the eastern districts of [[Norvik]]. It stretches far down all the way to the [[Gravity Flip]], and is the only discovered part of the City Below to do so. Known for its magical waterfalls and hanging architecture.

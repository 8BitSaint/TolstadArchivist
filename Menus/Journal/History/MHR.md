# Text
Stands for Mass [[Hag]] Recruitment. Was an initiative taken by several city-states during the [[Coast War]] to forcibly recruit and train young girls into hagdom.

# Notes
According to [[Lisa]], the MHR saw Hags imprisoned and fed young girls on masse, creating an entire generation of young [[Hagling|Haglings]].

Something about modifying either the hags or the haglings so the haglings would be less powerful and more controllable.

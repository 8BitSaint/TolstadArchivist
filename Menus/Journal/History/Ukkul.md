# Text
The jewel of [[Kargamesh]]. Means 'endless pillars' in primordial. A natural wonder of the world, an underground city where all elements live in harmony. Rivers of water and lava intertwine, and clouds gather around a forest of enormous stone pillars. A paradise far from the [[Coast]] and the [[Undercoast|Undersea]].

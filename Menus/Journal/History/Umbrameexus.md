# Text
The All-Wise one. The black [[dragon]] of [[Old Diminor]], known for holding the largest dragondom at his peak. His capital was the underground city of [[Silverslove]], constructed under the central marshland of the [[band]]. His strength was based on [[esoteric]] might, and he was known to carry a hundred [[human]] [[Wizard|wizards]] on his back when he went to war.

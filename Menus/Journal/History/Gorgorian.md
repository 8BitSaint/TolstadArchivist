# Text
A [[devil]] second in line to rule the deepest layer of hell. Legends say he was presented with a deal: Conquer one measly pocket dimension and in exchange he would be granted the throne. A millennia of fighting orcs, [[Elf|elves]], several [[Walking God|Walking Gods]] later, his corpse is now used as the foundation for [[Bloodcity Bay]].

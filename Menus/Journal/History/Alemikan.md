# Text
One of the last [[Urthfolk]] #Houses to arrive after the founding of [[Norvik]]. They originally went east during the [[Coast War]], but a handful of them survived and fled to Norvik with several [[Arcanist]] artifacts. Today the bloodline are highly revered in the city, the third Lord [[Patriarch Huggar]] having been of their bloodline.

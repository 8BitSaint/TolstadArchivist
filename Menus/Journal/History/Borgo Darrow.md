## Text
The first #Paladin of [[Urth]]. Founded the [[Urthguard]], as well as the illustrious [[Darrow]] bloodline. Known for his bravery, and inability to be damaged in battle. Died of heart attack at age 40. His passing is used as an example of the importance of aging men to keep track of cardiovascular diseases.

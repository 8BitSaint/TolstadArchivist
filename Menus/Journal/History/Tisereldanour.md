## Text
The Great Green Dragon. Supposedly the original green [[dragon]] on [[Jor]]. Her reign was long, and her fall ushered in the end of the [[Draconic Civil War]]. As well as the beginning of [[Arcanist]] rule in [[Old Diminor]]. She ruled in what is today [[Illym Forest]].

# Text
An event that took place during the mid-[[Arcane Era]], and lasted approximately fifty years. [[Arcanist|Arcanists]], in need of more manpower, organized mass immigrations from all corners of the [[Coast]]. All [[Human|Humans]], of any origin, were invited to join the various great magocratic cities.

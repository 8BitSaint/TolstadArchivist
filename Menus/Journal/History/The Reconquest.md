# Text
A historical event in [[Norvik]]. It began with [[Urth's Landing]] in 466 AE, and is said to have been finished by 468 AE, when [[Urth]] finished eradicating the [[Troll|trolls]] who roamed in the ruins of [[Toulin'catl]]. It is a point of contention if it should be called a 'reconquest' or just a 'conquest' as no [[Human|humans]] had ever lived in the location before.

# Notes
* A nickname for [[The Conquest]], mostly used by humans.

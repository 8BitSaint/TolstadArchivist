## Text
The united magocracy of the [[Hagginshags]] and [[Diminorii]]. This was arguably the most powerful of the [[Arcanist]]-states, said to rival the strength of warfare during the [[Gate Era]]. Their merger is considered one of the first bricks laid on the road to the [[Coast War]].

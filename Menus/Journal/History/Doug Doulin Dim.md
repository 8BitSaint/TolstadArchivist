# Text
The first [[human]] emperor on the [[Coast]]. Known as the Drake Slayer King, due to his slaying of several [[Dragon|Dragons]] and liberating their [[Human|humans]] slaves. Some call him Drake Doom Dealer Doug Doulin Dim. Most do not.

# Notes
* Founder of the magocratic [[Diminorii]] family of [[Old Diminor]].

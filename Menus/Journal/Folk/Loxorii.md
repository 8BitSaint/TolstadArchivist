# Text
Elephant folk. Known for the massive size, and ability to keep growing their entire lives. As part-[[Celestial|celestials]], most of them live in [[Divinii Torum]] and [[Ym]]. Although some are known to migrate peacefully into the valleys of [[Vyz-Kha|Vyz-Kha Peaks]] from their [[Girikut]] homeland. The [[Dwarf|dwarves]] generally have a very good relationship with them.

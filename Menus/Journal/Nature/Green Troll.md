## Text
Standing only slightly taller than the average [[human]], these [[Troll|trolls]] live among the moss and stone of [[Askan|Askanii]] woods. They are hunter-gatherers, and seldom organize into anything resembling civilization. Though they mostly avoid humans since [[the Conquest]], they are known to both attack travellers and raid settlements near their territory.

# Text
A [[monster]] native to [[Askan]]. They exist in broad categories, from small and mostly harmless [[Green Troll|green trolls]] to mutated volatile curse trolls. They vary in intelligence, strength, and organizational abilities, but they are a core enemy of the [[Urthfolk]]. It was [[Urth]] who destroyed their rising kingdom during the late [[Arcane Era]].

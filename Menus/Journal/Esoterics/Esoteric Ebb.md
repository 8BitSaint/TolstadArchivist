## Text
The draining of magic from [[Jor]]'s Realm. Theorized by the [[Arcanists]] during the end of the [[Coast War]], when their magic began to fail more and more.

An [[esoteric]] term for the draining of high tier magic from Jor's Realm. The lack of high tier magic is often taken for granted today, due to the general hatred of Arcanists, post-Coast War.

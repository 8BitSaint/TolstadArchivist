# Text
An [[esoteric]] relic left after the end of the [[Gate Era]]. There are eight of these pillars around the [[Coast]] (or four, if you believe the theories of a wrapping circular Coast!) and they are indestructible. Although there is some esoteric power within them, no one has been able to make use of them at all.

# Text
A rare 1st level [[Menus/Journal/Esoterics/Divination|Divination]] spell. It allows the user to notice illusions, traps, and other hidden and generally secret things within a certain range. What exactly constitutes a 'secret' is under debate, but it appears to differ on the caster's own interpretation of this word. The spell was created late in the Arcane Era by copying the powers from a [[Wand of Secrets]].

# Meta
**Difficulty Check:** Int DC 12
**Categories:** #Spells 

# Text
An 8th level #Enchantment spell. It deals a destructive psychic blow to the target's mind, leaving them in a demented state. In good cases, it can be reversed with a [[Greater Restoration]] spell. In bad cases, only a [[Wish]] spell can do anything to bring back the target from the abyss.

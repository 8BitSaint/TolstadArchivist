# Text
A 1st-level [[Menus/Journal/Esoterics/Divination|Divination]] spell, which allows the caster to see an [[esoteric]] effect around creatures who are not true neutral on the [[alignment]] axis. [[Cleric|Clerics]] often use this as a utility, even if alignments are not admissible in [[Norvik|Norvikian]] courts since the [[Urthfolk Rights Order]] of 499 AE.

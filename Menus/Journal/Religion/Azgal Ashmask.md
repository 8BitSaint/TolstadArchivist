## Text
A dwarf of a low caste born deep in the [[Lekm-Muraz]] tunnels. When he attained divinity in 443 AE, he crushed the kingdoms and formed the [[Azgal Folkhem Forge]]. On his death in 470 AE, a great civil war broke out, resulting in the exiling of all his followers to the surface. But his influence was felt all around the [[Undercoast]] and above, as his Exiled dwarves spread [[Azgalist|Azgalism]] to all corners of [[Jor]].

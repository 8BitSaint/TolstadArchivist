## Text
The term used to describe the actual gods, those who invaded the [[Coast]] during the [[Gate War]]. They hold no dominion on [[Jor]]'s Realm since [[the Closing of the Gates]], but the most common consensus among scholars is: all [[Walking God|Walking Gods]] are simply avatars of these real, outer ones.

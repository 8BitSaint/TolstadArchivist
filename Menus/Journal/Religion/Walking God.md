## Text
The term used to describe the [[divine]] avatars who rose and fell in and around the [[Divine Era]]. Most major religions on the [[Coast]] today are built around these figures, and their influence can be felt everywhere. [[Urth]] was the last known Walking God when he faded, twenty-seven years ago.

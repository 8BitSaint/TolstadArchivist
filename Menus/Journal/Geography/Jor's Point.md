# Text
The legendary first gate. Based on archeological evidence, the [[Beast of Jor|Beasts of Jor]] believes [[Jor]] to have first entered this realm at this location, at the farthest point of [[Jor's Ocean|Jor's Sea]], right where all the [[Band|bands]] collide. None who have tried sailing to this point have ever returned.

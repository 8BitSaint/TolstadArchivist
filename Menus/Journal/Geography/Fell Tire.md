# Intelligence
## Text
One of eight [[Band|bands]] along the [[Coast]]. A land half-ruined from millenia of warfare, its red lowlands stands for a quarter of the all food production on the Coast, all flowing from the trade ports in [[Bloodcity Bay]]. The name 'Fell Tire' either originates in Orcish as 'High Place', or "Bad Fort" in Infernal. Or "Hill Land" in [[Goblin]]. It is hotly debated.

# Wisdom
## Text
A land of [[Elf|Elven]] ruins with magically fertile lowlands in between. In its center sits Bloodcity Bay, the largest city on the Coast, built out of the corpse (or rather just the head) of [[Gorgorian]].

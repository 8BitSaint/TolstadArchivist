# Text
One of eight [[Band|bands]] along the [[Coast]]. Means either 'Growing Maw' or 'Into the Air' in abyssal. A deeply cursed jungle hellscape filled with volcanic rivers, flesheating insects, and murderous plants. Once an [[Elf|Elven]] sanctuary, now home to [[Demon|demonic]] warlords, [[Tiefling|Tieflings]] cities, and [[celestial]] crusaders.

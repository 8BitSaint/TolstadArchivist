# Text
One of eight [[Band|bands]] along the [[Coast]]. Great homeland of the elusive and isolationist high [[Elf|Elves]]. A land of artificial forests, crags covered in eternal darkness, and roots burrowing into the very Coast itself. The Ym have fought a losing battle against [[Devil|devils]] and [[Celestial|celestials]] for millenia, but their authoritarian society still lives within those great walls.

# Text
The most populated city around the [[Coast]]. Built upon the corpse of [[Gorgorian]] the Great and Terrible. A sprawling mess of wooden walkways and buildings carved out of the calcified remains. Once famous for its fleet of airships deployed during the [[Coast War]]. Known as the origin of the [[Industrial Revolution]], and while [[New Thazen]] has overtaken it as the leader in manufacturing, it still stands as one of the most industrious city states.

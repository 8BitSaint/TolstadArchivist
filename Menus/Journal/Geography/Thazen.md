## Text
The greatest of the floating cities. Center of the [[Haagin-Dim Coalition]] and captured essence of the nameless [[Fourth Apprentice]] of [[Jor]]. It was the most [[Esoteric|esoterically]] powerful city state in history, and famous for its shadowy thousand feet long tentacles that could be seen in the skies for a hundred miles away. It fell in the year 481 AE. It was rebuilt, now on the ground, in the decades after.

# Text
A highly dysfunctional pickaxe. It's supposed to point towards the nearest "secret"; a trap, fake wall, or somewhere where other dungeoneering goodies are hidden. However, when you wield it, it just keeps trying to point upwards, making it impossible to actually use. 

Though the runes look quite interesting ...

# Effects
+1 #Strength
+1 #Intelligence

# Obtaining
1. Go to [[Game World/Visitable Locations/The City Below The City|The City Below The City]] and walk through the north doorway
2. Follow the path to the East until you find [[Akzel Madsson]]
3. Complete his quest

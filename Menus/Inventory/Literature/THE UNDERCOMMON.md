# Text
A popular fan-[[Magazine|zine]]. Shoddily printed and filled with letters, fanfiction, and reviews. It's essentially a collection of writing from various fans of local theatre shows, musical artists, and nerdy activities. This issue seems to be full of info about [[Larper|LARPING]], which is taking the youth by storm.

The zine is infamous for accidentally releasing issues in an enchanted indestructible paper. A #legal battle is being fought over the difficulty in removing them from circulation. You cannot destroy this magazine no matter what you do.

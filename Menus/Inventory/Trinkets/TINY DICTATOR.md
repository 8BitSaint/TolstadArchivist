# Text
An unseen servant magically attuned to a feather pencil and paper - it will write down everything spoken within 10 feet. The content diseappars of the page as it writes. The servant can follow commands to write down specific topics, keep the notes in a certain style, or focus on a chosen person's words.

These are very common in the halls of [[the Palace]], where words can ruin lives - or worse: careers.

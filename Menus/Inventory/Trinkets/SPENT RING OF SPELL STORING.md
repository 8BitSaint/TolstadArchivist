# Text
A compact ring tingles with [[esoteric]] might. It is ancient, made entirely out of brass, and inside the top-shell you can feel - you know - there is a tiny scroll. However, that scroll has been been long since spent on some [[divine]] spell. How do you know that? Again- you can feel it.

It's original use is long lost. But keeping it around seems ... good. Perhaps its previous owner was a good man, if he left it behind in the [[Temple of Urth]].

# Text
The cost and spell slot requirement of '[[Sending]]' has created many off-brand versions capable of short-distance messaging. During the [[Coast War]], the [[Arcanist]] forces were often equipped with this so-called 'Pebble', a stone capable of casting Sending on any familiar target within a [[Meters|kilometer]].

# Text
> A dwarf named [[Akzel Madsson]] is looking for a legendary lost mine, somewhere in the tunnel to the southwest. But the [[Halfway Bridge Tribe|HWBT]] won't allow him passage.

> Find a symbol of the sun, or lack there of 'the shrine', then report back to him to get a reward.

> You found an ancient cat-themed shrine, which gave you a poem.

> Akzel realized he was looking in the wrong place. But the poem seemed to have given him a clue where to search next - he invites you to join him in a couple of months for a proper expedition.

# Completing
1. Proceed to [[Game World/Visitable Locations/The City Below The City|The City Below The City]] 
2. Walk through the north doorway, then proceed East until you find a room with [[Akzel Madsson]].
3. Speak to him and accept his quest
4. Speak with [[Lady Sageleaf]] to move forward [[The Tea Shop That Blew Up]] so you can access [[Roots]]
5. Proceed through Roots into the [[South Caverns]]
6. Find the Shrine
7. ???
8. Report back to Akzel for the reward

# Reward

* Examine the item to learn [[Detect Secrets]] by studying the pickaxe

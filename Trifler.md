# Text
A rare class of [[aberration]] found across the [[Coast]]. They are known to run various cults across the [[Undersea Coast|Undersea]], often trading in information. Known to inhibit powerful psionics, they are very dangerous. A Trifler named [[Horseman]] runs a major guild in [[Middle Edge]], one of the few operating publically.
